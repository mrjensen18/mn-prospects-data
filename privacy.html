MN Prospects app privacy policy

Minnesota Prospects Baseball Tournaments · Effective October 22, 2026

The MN Prospects app shows schedules, scores, brackets, standings, rosters and field status for Minnesota Prospects Baseball Tournaments events. This policy explains what the app does with information.

No accounts and no personal information

You don't sign up or log in. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we do not collect them.

What stays on your phone

Teams and players you follow, and the last tournament data the app downloaded, are saved only on your device so the app works offline. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Field and weather alerts

If you turn on alerts, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver them. Firebase gives your device a random notification ID and records which alert lists it joined: the general field & weather list, plus one list for each team you follow, so you only get reschedule alerts for those teams. This ID is not linked to your name or any other personal information, and it is used only to deliver alerts. You can turn alerts off at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → MN Prospects. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

Tournament data

The app downloads public tournament information from GitHub each time it opens. As with any website, GitHub may log technical details of that request, such as your IP address, under the GitHub privacy statement. Team rosters shown in the app are provided by the teams for tournament and recruiting purposes.

Links to other sites

Registration, GameChanger, hotel and social media links open other websites or apps, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is for players, parents, coaches and fans of youth baseball. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

No ads, tracking or selling data

The app has no advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell or share data.

Changes and contact
